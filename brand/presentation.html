What we heard

The brief

A free QR code generator that runs entirely in the browser: no sign-up, no tracking, and static codes that keep working even if the site goes away. It should feel honest, private, quick and durable, without the category's chain links, arrows, padlocks and phones.

honestprivatequickdurable

Success: owns one simple idea, reads at 16 px in a browser tab, works in one colour, and stands apart from blue QR generators.

Final design

Escape Q

A QR code's corner eye whose centre square steps out through the corner, becoming the tail of a Q.

  • Built from the QR code's own finder pattern, not a picture of a phone or a chain
  • The escaping square is what the product does: your content leaves as a code that works anywhere
  • The same square is the Q's tail in the wordmark, so symbol and name share one detail
  • Escape Orange stands apart from the category's blues and keeps 3.6 : 1 or more on white, paper and dark
